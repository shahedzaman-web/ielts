It is believed that technological advancement has reduced the gap between people; however, others think that it has moved us apart. This essay will discuss both views before explaining why I believe technology has brought people together.

Technological advancement has made communication and travelling effortless and faster. People can now communicate with anyone with just a tap of a button because of smartphones, computers and other technology. Through advances in communication, people are connected to their friends and family. In addition, travelling has become effortless and quick; therefore, people can meet their relatives and loved ones more frequently. This reduces the long-distance gap. For example, in the previous century, communication was too difficult: people had to write letters to send a message or important news to their relatives or friends and also wait for their response, which took months. But nowadays it has become effortless and quicker.

On the other hand, even though communication and travel make everything easier and quicker, they have also driven us apart by replacing deep, physical contact with shallow digital interactions. Nowadays many people spend hours staring at screens instead of engaging in physical, co-located conversations. Simple text messages and short-form replies substitute for emotional depth and non-verbal interaction like tone, eye contact, and body language. Even though communication technology has advanced, it has lost the emotional connection. However, this trend can be overcome by visiting friends and family more frequently and arranging more frequent social gatherings.

In conclusion, although technology is limited by the limitations of physical and emotional connection, it has also brought us much closer through advanced communication technology such as smartphones, computers, laptops and the internet.

Model answer (Band 7.5+ style)

It is often argued that modern technology has either united people or isolated them. While I accept that digital tools can create distance, I believe that, overall, technology has brought people closer together.

On the one hand, critics of modern technology make a persuasive point. Smartphones and social media can replace face-to-face conversation with shallow digital interaction. Many people now spend hours scrolling through screens instead of talking to those around them, and short text messages often lack tone, eye contact and body language. This can weaken emotional depth and leave individuals feeling lonely even when they are constantly “connected”. For example, a family may sit together in the same room while each member stares at a separate device, which is a clear illustration of physical proximity without genuine interaction.

On the other hand, technology has dramatically reduced distance in other ways. Instant messaging, video calls and affordable travel allow people to maintain relationships across cities and continents. Migrant workers can speak to their children daily, and friends can share important moments in real time. Moreover, online communities provide support for people who might otherwise be isolated, such as those with rare illnesses or disabilities. In this sense, technology creates connections that would have been impossible a few decades ago.

In conclusion, although digital communication can sometimes replace deeper physical contact, I believe the overall effect of modern technology has been to bring people together. The key is to use it deliberately: maintain face-to-face relationships while taking advantage of the extraordinary ways technology allows us to stay connected.

  4. Develop the counter-view: add one concrete example to the paragraph about isolation.
  5. Split long sentences: do not join four ideas with “and also”; use full stops and linkers.
