HOW TO IMPROVE FLUENCY

Q1. When do people like to read the newspaper?

Many people like to read the newspaper in the morning, often with breakfast or a cup of tea. Some busy people only read it when something important happens, like a disaster or major news event. But generally, reading the paper is a daily habit for many.

Q2. How important is it for people to have a choice of newspaper?

It’s very important. Different newspapers focus on different topics and have different perspectives. Having choices allows people to get a balanced view and find the content that interests them most. For example, some may prefer local news while others want international coverage.

Q3. What does a 'good' newspaper contain?

A good newspaper should contain daily updates on important national and international events. It should also include weather forecasts, environmental news, and warnings about natural disasters. In addition, it should cover achievements in sports and culture, and ideally reflect the interests of its readers.

Q4. Why do some people choose to read the news on the Internet rather than in a newspaper?

Many people prefer online news because it’s convenient and accessible. Most people have smartphones, so they can read news whenever they want, even while commuting. Online news is often free, although some sites require a subscription. It’s also updated constantly, so readers can get the latest information quickly.

Q5. How is Internet news different from the news you read in the newspaper?

Internet news is often more immediate and interactive. It can include videos, links, and social media updates. Newspapers, on the other hand, are more static and provide a summary of the day’s events. Online news also allows readers to comment and share stories, which traditional newspapers cannot do.

Q6. Will Internet news ever replace newspapers? [Why? / Why not?]

I think internet news will become even more popular in the future, thanks to technological advancements. However, I don’t think it will completely replace printed newspapers. Some people still prefer the experience of reading a physical paper, and printed newspapers are often seen as more reliable. So, while online news will grow, printed newspapers will probably continue to exist.
