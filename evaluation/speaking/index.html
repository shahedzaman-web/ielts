Q5. How do you think school life differs from university life?

University life is much more independent. Students have the freedom to choose their own courses and join any organisation they like, whereas at school the curriculum is fixed. University also offers more opportunities, like living in dormitories and attending various events. The academic pressure is different too – university requires more self-directed study.

Q6. How important do you think it is for individuals to carry on learning after they have finished school and university?

It’s extremely important. The world is constantly changing, and there’s always more to learn. Lifelong learning helps people stay updated, develop new skills, and pursue interests they didn’t have time for earlier. Whether it’s through online courses, reading, or workshops, continuing to learn keeps the mind active and can lead to better career opportunities.
