Final Tips for Using This Vocabulary

Vocabulary only works when it's used naturally inside a well-structured answer.

1Don't memorise lists — learn words in context through definitions and practice.
2Practise 5–7 new words per day in your own answers.
3Record yourself answering Part 3 questions using this vocabulary.
4Focus on collocations — they make your English sound natural.
5Use Bangladeshi examples — Cox's Bazar, Dhaka, Eid, garment industry, monsoon.
6Balance perspectives — use discourse markers like "Having said that," "On the other hand," "Provided that."
7Aim for 35–60 seconds per answer with a clear opinion, reason and example.
8Self-correct naturally — if you make a mistake, rephrase without panic.
