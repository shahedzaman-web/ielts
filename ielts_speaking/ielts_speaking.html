The Band 7–8 Answer Formula (Natural Speech Edition)

Part 1 · 3–4 bullets
  • 1. Direct answer, straight away
  • 2. One concrete reason or detail
  • 3. A conditional or contrast ("If I had to pick…", "That said…")
  • 4. One personal / Bangladeshi example
  • 5. Stop naturally — don't speechify
Part 2 · 4–5 bullets
  • 1. Open with the topic in one line
  • 2. Cover all four cue bullets
  • 3. Add sensory / concrete detail
  • 4. Use past tenses for the narrative
  • 5. End with a feeling, reflection or conditional
Part 3 · 2–3 bullets
  • 1. Answer first, then explain
  • 2. WHY → EXAMPLE → BALANCE
  • 3. Compare groups carefully
  • 4. Use "That said…", "It depends…", "If we look at…"
  • 5. Close with "So overall…"

Useful Part 3 language

I'd argue that…I tend to think… The main reason is…This is largely because… For instance…A good example would be… That said…Having said that… To some extent…It depends on… So overall, I think…On balance…
