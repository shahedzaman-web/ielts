What Is Subject–Verb Agreement?

Subject-verb agreement is the grammatical rule that the verb in a sentence must match the subject in number and person. In English, the verb primarily needs to match the number — singular or plural.

💡 Core Rule: If the subject is singular, the verb must be singular. If the subject is plural, the verb must be plural.

✅ Singular

The boy runs fast.

Singular subject + singular verb

✅ Plural

The boys run fast.

Plural subject + plural verb

Subject–Verb Agreement Rules

Here are the essential rules you need to know, with clear examples for each.

1 Singular ↔ Plural

A singular subject takes a singular verb; a plural subject takes a plural verb.

Rachel spends her free time listening to music.
Blaine and Kurt play the piano.
She likes dessert after every meal.

2 The Verb Be

The verb be changes based on both number and person.

PersonPronounVerbExample
First singularIamI am confident.
First pluralWeareWe are confident.
SecondYouareYou are confident.
Third singularHe / She / ItisShe is a singer.
Third pluralTheyareThey are confident.
The girls are waiting for you.
I was reading the latest book.
💡 Note: “Every boy and every girl was given a bar of chocolate.”

3 Have vs. Has

Has → third-person singular (he, she, it, singular names). Have → I, you, we, they, and all plural subjects.

I have a younger brother.
Swetha has a pet dog.
Finn has been waiting to talk to you.

4 Compound Subjects with And

Two or more subjects joined by and take a plural verb.

Krish and Radha are on their way to the airport.
Caren, Sheela and Akash have completed their assessments.

5 Or / Nor – Proximity Rule

With or or nor, the verb agrees with the subject closest to it.

Celery or spring onion works fine.
The teacher or the students are coming.
Neither Ricky nor Gina is here yet.

6 Indefinite Pronouns

Words like anybody, everyone, nobody, somebody are singular.

Everybody loves using correct grammar!
Nobody has understood anything.
Everyone was happy with the outcome.

7 Either…or / Neither…nor

The verb agrees with the noun or pronoun that comes just before the verb.

Neither the children nor their parents are aware.
Either the teacher or the students have to take initiative.

8 Tricky Nouns

Some nouns look plural but are singular; others are always plural.

Singular in meaning

The news has been depressing.
Mathematics is my favorite subject.

Plural in meaning

The police have been looking.
The scissors do not work.

9 Negative Sentences

Use do with plural subjects and I, you, we, they. Use does with third-person singular.

The children do not like trigonometry.
My father does not work at the bank anymore.

10 Interrogative Sentences

The helping verb at the beginning must agree with the subject.

Do you read thriller novels?
Does she know you already?
Has Sharon submitted her project?

11 Sentences Beginning with Here / There

The subject follows the verb, so the verb agrees with the real subject that comes after.

Here is your book.
There are new maps in the DLC.
There have been many changes.

12 Abstract & Uncountable Nouns

They are considered singular and take a singular verb.

Honesty is the best policy.
Love makes people do crazy things.

13 Time, Distance & Money

When taken as a whole, use a singular verb.

1,267 kilometres is too long for us.
10 years is not considered optimum.
₹1,000 is a little too much.

14 Collective Nouns

Can be singular (acting together) or plural (acting separately).

The team goes to practice at 4:00. (together)
The team go to their own homes. (separately)
My family is settled in Australia.

15 All / Some / A lot of

The verb agrees with the noun that follows the preposition.

All of my dresses have become tight.
A lot of food is left out.
Some of the books are torn.

16 Each / Every

When used as the subject, they are singular.

Each student has been asked to provide a letter.
Every teacher, parent and student is expected to work together.

17 Wishes & Requests

Subjunctive mood uses were for all subjects in wishes.

I wish I were a bird.
If you were here, I would not be sad.
We request that everyone make their choices now.
📌
Summary: The verb must always match the subject in number. Watch out for tricky subjects like collective nouns, indefinite pronouns, and phrases that come between.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is subject-verb agreement?

Subject-verb agreement is the grammatical rule that the subject and verb in a sentence must use the same number and person. With the exception of be, in English it's about matching the number.

How does subject-verb agreement work?

If the subject is singular, use the singular form of the verb; if plural, use the plural form. The only exception is be, which changes based on number and person.

What are examples of subject-verb agreement?

Cats make great pets. → plural subject + plural verb.
A cat makes a great pet. → singular subject + singular verb.

Why is subject-verb agreement important?

It ensures clarity and correctness in writing and speech. Errors can confuse readers and make your writing seem less professional.
