📘 How to Answer Each Question Type

The structure you should follow for every Task 2 essay type

✅ Agree / Disagree

State your position clearly in the introduction. Acknowledge the other side briefly, then argue your view.

Intro: position → Body 1: main reason → Body 2: second reason + counter → Conclusion

⚖️ Discuss Both Views

You must cover both sides fairly. Your opinion should appear in the intro and conclusion (and ideally in one body paragraph).

Intro: both views + opinion → Body 1: view A → Body 2: view B → Conclusion

➕➖ Advantages / Disadvantages

If the question asks "outweigh", you must decide. List both sides, then clearly state which is stronger.

Intro: state which outweighs → Body 1: stronger side → Body 2: weaker side → Conclusion

👍👎 Positive / Negative

Take a position. Support with reasons and examples. If you say "mostly positive", concede one negative clearly.

Intro: position → Body 1: main positives → Body 2: negatives → Conclusion

🔧 Problem / Solution

Identify 2 causes/problems in one body paragraph and 2 solutions in the other. Solutions must link to the problems.

Intro: restate → Body 1: causes → Body 2: solutions → Conclusion

❓ Two-Part Question

You must answer both questions explicitly. Give equal weight to each part.

Intro: address both questions → Body 1: answer Q1 → Body 2: answer Q2 → Conclusion
💡

Universal Task 2 principles

  • Word count: aim for 260–290 words — enough to develop ideas without rambling.
  • Paragraphing: always 4 paragraphs (intro, 2 body, conclusion).
  • Position: state and maintain a clear opinion throughout — do not sit on the fence unless the question allows it.
  • Cohesion: use a range of linkers (Furthermore, However, Consequently, By contrast) but avoid mechanical overuse.
  • Examples: one specific example per body paragraph is better than three vague ones.
